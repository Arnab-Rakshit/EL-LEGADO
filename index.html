Selection Process

The zone always shrinks.

Five stages, one shrinking circle of contenders — 256 down to 8. Nothing about a player's starting group decides where they finish. Only results do.

256Players
Registration Phase

Phase 0 — The Field

256 players are selected from all applications and divided into 4 groups of 64. Each group forms 16 squads of 4 players. Every player begins with a clean slate — no seniority, no reputation, no shortcuts.

192Advance
Phase 1

Squad Qualification

Players compete in their assigned squads. Rankings are based on official tournament points and overall performance.

Top 32 squads (128 players) qualify automatically
64 wildcards from the bottom 32 squads, on personal stats
Wildcards must include the top 16 IGLs from eliminated squads

Total qualified: 192 players. All others are eliminated.

96Advance
Phase 2

Individual Ranking

Team results no longer decide a player's fate. Every player is now judged on their own merit.

  • Combat Efficiency
  • Damage Output
  • Survival
  • Rotation
  • Utility Usage
  • Communication
  • Decision Making
  • Consistency
Top 96 individual players qualify
Top 24 IGLs remain eligible to continue
64Advance
Phase 3

Solo Assessment

Pure individual skill, under pressure, alone. No teammates to cover a mistake.

  • Mechanical Skill
  • Game Sense
  • Positioning
  • Clutch Ability
  • Adaptability
  • Survival
Top 48 players advance
Top 16 IGLs advance

Total remaining: 64 players enter the Development Camp.

Rules of Engagement

Core principles.

Every rule below applies equally to every player, at every stage, with no exceptions made for reputation.

I.
Performance over popularity Your name means nothing here. Your points do.
II.
Discipline over talent Raw skill gets you noticed. Discipline gets you selected.
III.
Growth over excuses Every stage is designed to expose weaknesses so they can be fixed.
IV.
Every match matters There is no phase where results don't count.
V.
Every player can earn their place Wildcards, IGL protections, and Academy promotions all exist for this reason.
VI.
No roster spot is guaranteed Not for the finalists. Not for the Main Roster. Not ever.

Legends aren't recruited.
